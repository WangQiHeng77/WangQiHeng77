## Qiheng Wang

M.Sc. student in Computer Control & Automation at Nanyang Technological University, with a dissertation supervised by Jianfei Yang. B.Eng. in Automation, Beijing Institute of Technology.

I study how learned robot systems keep working beyond their training conditions: execution speed and timing, perception under degraded images, and physical decisions that can be checked. I am looking for robotics PhD positions starting in Fall 2027.
